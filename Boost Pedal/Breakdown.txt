This was designed off of schematics I found to get a better feeling of KiCad and LTSpice. This is no way optimized at all there isn't even switch and haha more or less proof of concept and for my personal enjoyment. It's not pretty but it works

Power-section
D1: Reverse Polarity protection
C1: Bulk Capacitor
R1 & R2: Voltage divider creating 4.5V
C2: Coupling Capacitor to filter bias noise

Main Circuit
R3 & R4: R3 Acts as a bias resistors. R3 acts as pull down resistor and R4 pulls up setting impedance at 5 mega ohms. 
C3: Coupling input Capacitor  
R5: Current limiting resistor
U1:Opamp(TL071)
C4: Feedback Capacitor 
R6, R8, R7: R6 and R8 Helps set gain with R7 supposed to represent a Gain potentiometer 
C6:Output coupling capacitor 
R9: Current limiting resistor to set output impedance
R10: Pull down resistor that's a stand in for 10k volume pot 

Non- Switching 6.3mm jack- For first design and simplicity sake the NJ2FD-V was chosen. The down side is that pedal needs to be turned off completely and interference when disconnected.
